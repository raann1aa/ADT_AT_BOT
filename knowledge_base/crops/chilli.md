# Chilli / Mirch (मिर्च) Cultivation Guide

## Agro-Climatic Window and Seedlings
Chilli (*Capsicum annuum*) is heavily cultivated in Andhra Pradesh (Guntur), Telangana, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, and Maharashtra.
- **Sowing Seasons:**
  - Kharif: Nursery in May-June, transplant in July-August.
  - Rabi: Nursery in September-October, transplant in October-November.
  - Summer: Nursery in January, transplant in February.
- **Seed Rate:** 70 to 100 grams per acre for high-yielding hybrids (Teja, Armoor, US-341, Guntur Sannam).
- **Transplanting:** 30-35 day old seedlings, dipped in solution of Imidacloprid (0.5 ml/L) + Carbendazim (1 g/L) for 15 minutes before planting to provide 20 days systemic protection against early sucking pests.

## Spacing and Bed Layout
- Paired row planting on raised beds with drip irrigation and 25-micron silver-black plastic mulch:
  - Bed top width: 80 cm, furrow width: 40 cm.
  - Zigzag planting: 45 cm plant-to-plant, 60 cm between two rows on the same bed.

## Fertilizer Schedule (Per Acre)
1. **Basal Dose:** 10 tonnes FYM, 50 kg SSP, 25 kg MOP, 10 kg Micronutrient mixture, 20 kg Neem Cake.
2. **Drip Fertigation (Weekly Schedule):**
   - Growth phase (1-30 DAT): 19:19:19 @ 3 kg/acre twice a week.
   - Flowering phase (31-60 DAT): 12:61:00 @ 3 kg + 13:00:45 @ 4 kg/acre.
   - Fruiting & Picking phase (61 DAT onwards): 00:52:34 @ 4 kg + Calcium Nitrate @ 3 kg alternate weeks.

## Preventing Flower and Fruit Drop (फूल और फल झड़ना)
Commonly caused by high temperatures (>35°C), water stress, or hormone imbalance:
1. Spray **Alpha Naphthyl Acetic Acid 4.5 SL (Planofix)** @ 0.25 ml per 4.5 liters of water (approx 4 ml in 15-liter knapsack sprayer) at peak flowering.
   - *Warning:* Overdosing Planofix causes burning of tips and premature senescence. Never exceed 4 ml per 15 L water!
2. Spray Nitrobenzene 20% @ 2 ml/L or seaweed extract @ 2 ml/L to stimulate bud retention.
3. Foliar spray of Chelate Boron (20%) @ 1 g/L at initiation of flowering.
