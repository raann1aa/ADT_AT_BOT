# Cotton (कपास) Cultivation Guide

## Overview and Soil Requirements
Cotton (*Gossypium hirsutum*) is the premier cash crop grown in Gujarat, Maharashtra, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Punjab, and Haryana.
- **Soil Type:** Deep, well-drained Black Cotton soil (Regur soil) with high moisture retention capacity and pH 7.0 to 8.5.
- **Sowing Period:** May 15 to June 15 under irrigated conditions; late June to early July with the arrival of the monsoon in rainfed areas.
- **Spacing:** 90 cm × 60 cm for Bt hybrids; 120 cm × 45 cm for dense branching types.

## Fertilizer Recommendations (Per Acre)
For Bt Cotton hybrids (recommended 60:30:30 kg N:P:K per acre):
1. **Basal Dose (At Sowing):**
   - Single Super Phosphate (SSP): 100 kg OR DAP: 35 kg
   - MOP: 25 kg
   - Magnesium Sulphate: 10 kg (prevents leaf reddening)
2. **Top Dressing 1 (Square Formation Stage - 30 to 35 DAS):**
   - Urea: 30 kg + Ammonium Sulphate: 15 kg
3. **Top Dressing 2 (Peak Flowering Stage - 60 to 65 DAS):**
   - Urea: 35 kg + MOP: 15 kg
4. **Foliar Sprays (Boll Development Stage):**
   - Spray 13:00:45 (Potassium Nitrate) @ 10 g/liter of water at 75 and 90 DAS to prevent boll dropping and increase boll size.
   - 1% Magnesium Sulphate spray if lower leaves show purple/red discoloration.

## Weed and Canopy Management
- Keep field weed-free during the critical 15 to 60 DAS window.
- Terminal clipping (topping): Detop the main stem apex at 75-80 days or at 4.5 feet height to curb vegetative overgrowth and direct nutrients to boll filling.
