# Gram / Chickpea (चना) Cultivation Guide

## Agro-Ecological Requirements
Chickpea (*Cicer arietinum*) is the major Rabi pulse crop grown in Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, UP, and Karnataka.
- **Sowing Window:** October 15 to November 15. Delayed sowing increases incidence of pod borer and terminal heat stress.
- **Soil:** Deep black soils or well-drained alluvial loams. Avoid soils prone to waterlogging.
- **Seed Rate:**
  - Desi varieties (JG 11, Vijay, Digvijay, Jakhi 9218): 25 to 30 kg/acre.
  - Kabuli varieties (KAK 2, PKV Kabuli 2, Dollar): 40 to 45 kg/acre.
- **Sowing Depth:** Sow at 8 to 10 cm deep to place seeds in moist zone and reduce collar rot/wilt incidence.

## Seed Treatment and Bio-fertilizers
1. Treat seed with *Trichoderma viride* @ 4 g/kg or Carbendazim 12% + Mancozeb 63% (SAAF) @ 2 g/kg seed to protect against *Fusarium* wilt.
2. Inoculate with *Mesorhizobium ciceri* culture @ 10 g/kg and PSB culture @ 10 g/kg seed.

## Fertilizer Recommendation (Per Acre)
- **Basal Dose:** DAP: 35-40 kg + MOP: 15 kg + Sulphur: 10 kg.
- Pulses do not require top-dressed urea. Excess nitrogen induces vegetative growth at the expense of flowering.
- Foliar spray of 2% Urea or 19:19:19 @ 5 g/L at flower initiation and pod filling stage significantly increases grain size.

## Nipping / Topping (खुंटाई)
- Pluck the tender apical shoots (top 2-3 cm) at 30 to 40 DAS when plants are 15-20 cm tall.
- **Benefits:** Suppresses apical dominance, triggers massive lateral branching, doubles number of pods per plant, and harvested tender leaves are consumed as tasty *Chana Saag*.
- Avoid nipping if field is infested with wilt or moisture is deficient.

## Irrigation Schedule
Gram is mostly grown rainfed on residual soil moisture, but 1-2 critical irrigations boost yields by 40-50%:
1. **Pre-flowering / Branching (40-45 DAS):** Crucial for canopy growth.
2. **Pod Development Stage (65-75 DAS):** Prevents grain shriveling.
- **Warning:** NEVER irrigate during peak flowering stage, as it causes massive flower drop!
