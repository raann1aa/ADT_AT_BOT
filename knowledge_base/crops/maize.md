# Maize / Corn (मक्का) Cultivation Guide

## Seasons and Seed Rate
Maize (*Zea mays*) is widely cultivated across Karnataka, Bihar, MP, Rajasthan, Maharashtra, and Andhra Pradesh.
- **Kharif Sowing:** June 15 to July 10 (onset of monsoon).
- **Rabi Maize (Highest yield potential):** October 15 to November 15 (yields reach 35-45 quintals/acre).
- **Seed Rate:** 7 to 8 kg per acre for single cross grain hybrids; 10 kg per acre for sweet corn/baby corn.
- **Spacing:** 60 cm row-to-row, 20 cm plant-to-plant (target 30,000 to 33,000 healthy plants per acre).

## Fertilizer Schedule (Per Acre)
Maize is an exhaustive feeder of nutrients:
- **Basal Dose (In furrows before seed drop):** 35 kg DAP, 30 kg MOP, 10 kg Zinc Sulphate (21%).
- **First Top Dressing (Knee-high stage - 25 to 30 DAS):** 40 kg Urea along with weeding and earthing up.
- **Second Top Dressing (Tasseling / Silking stage - 50 to 55 DAS):** 35 kg Urea + 10 kg MOP.
- **Grain Filling Spray:** Foliar spray of 13:00:45 @ 10 g/L at early blister stage ensures bold, well-filled cobs till the tip.

## Critical Irrigation Stages
If monsoon fails or in Rabi maize:
1. **Knee-high Stage (25-30 DAS):** Rapid vegetative growth.
2. **Tasseling Stage (45-50 DAS):** Critical! Moisture stress reduces pollen viability.
3. **Silking Stage (55-60 DAS):** Moisture stress leads to poor seed set and barren cobs.
4. **Grain Milking Stage (70-75 DAS):** Determines test weight and kernel starch.
