# Mustard / Sarson (सरसों) Cultivation Guide

## Sowing Schedule and Soil
Mustard (*Brassica juncea*) is the principal Rabi oilseed in Rajasthan, Haryana, UP, MP, and Punjab.
- **Sowing Period:** October 1 to October 25 (optimum day temp 25-28°C). Avoid late sowing beyond November 5 as it exposes flowering to devastating mustard aphid attacks.
- **Soil:** Loam to sandy loam soils with good drainage; tolerates slight salinity.
- **Seed Rate:** 1.5 to 2.0 kg per acre.
- **Spacing & Thinning (छंटाई):** Row spacing 45 cm; thin seedlings at 15-20 DAS to keep 10-15 cm spacing between plants. One healthy plant per spot prevents spindly stems and promotes heavy branching.

## Fertilizer Management (Per Acre)
Mustard is extremely responsive to **Sulphur**, which directly enhances oil content by 2-3%:
- **Basal Dose:** 50 kg SSP (Single Super Phosphate provides 8 kg P and 5.5 kg S), 15 kg Urea, 15 kg MOP, and 10 kg Bentonite Sulphur (90%).
- **First Top Dressing (At first irrigation, 25-30 DAS):** 35 kg Urea.
- Avoid excessive nitrogen which delays maturity and makes crops susceptible to lodging and aphid infestation.

## Irrigation Management
Mustard has low water requirement (2 irrigations):
1. **First Irrigation:** 28 to 35 DAS at late vegetative/flower primordia stage. Follow with light intercultural weeding.
2. **Second Irrigation:** 55 to 65 DAS at siliqua (pod) formation stage.

## Protecting Mustard from Frost (पाला)
When night temperatures drop below 4°C in late December to mid-January:
1. Apply light irrigation to the field in the evening (wet soil retains heat).
2. Create smoke by burning dry weeds/trash on field bunds on the windward side.
3. Foliar spray of 0.1% dilute Sulphuric Acid (1 ml concentrated H2SO4 per liter water) or soluble Sulphur 80% WDG @ 2 g/L before the frost forecast.
