# Onion (प्याज) Cultivation Guide

## Growing Seasons and Varieties
1. **Kharif Onion:** Sown June-July, transplanted July-August, harvested October-November (Varieties: Agrifound Dark Red, Baswant 780, Bhima Super).
2. **Late Kharif (Rangda):** Sown August-September, transplanted October-November, harvested January-February.
3. **Rabi Onion (Best for Storage):** Sown October-November, transplanted December-January, harvested April-May (Varieties: N-2-4-1, Bhima Shakti, Agrifound Light Red).

## Nursery and Transplanting
- **Seed Rate:** 4 to 5 kg per acre.
- **Nursery Area:** 5-6 gunthas (approx. 500-600 sq. meters) on raised beds (15 cm high, 1.2 m wide).
- **Transplanting Age:** 40 to 45 days old seedlings (pencil thickness). Clip seedling tips by 1/3rd to reduce transpiration shock.
- **Spacing:** 15 cm row-to-row and 10 cm plant-to-plant on flat beds or broad bed furrows (BBF).

## Fertilizer Schedule (Per Acre)
Onions are heavy feeders of Nitrogen, Potash, and Sulphur.
1. **Basal Dose (Before Transplanting):**
   - 10 tonnes well-rotted FYM or 2 tonnes Vermicompost
   - DAP: 50 kg OR NPK 10:26:26: 75 kg
   - MOP: 35 kg
   - **Bentonite Sulphur (90%): 15 kg** (Crucial for pungency, shelf life, and skin luster)
2. **First Top Dressing (30 Days After Transplanting - DAT):**
   - Urea: 30 kg + Micronutrient mixture (Zinc, Boron)
3. **Second Top Dressing (45-50 DAT):**
   - Urea: 25 kg + 00:52:34 foliar spray @ 5 g/L
4. **Bulb Enlargement Stage (60-75 DAT):**
   - Do NOT apply excess nitrogen as it causes thick necks (ढोल्या/double bulbs) and rot.
   - Foliar spray of 00:00:50 (Potassium Sulphate) @ 5 g/L + Boron (20%) @ 1 g/L to enhance bulb weight, firmness, and uniform color.

## Water Management and Curing
- Maintain uniform moisture; do not allow soil to dry out cracking during bulb expansion.
- **Stop irrigation 10-15 days before harvest** when 50% of the tops naturally bend and fall over (neck fall stage).
- **Field Curing:** Leave harvested bulbs in windrows covered with foliage for 3-5 days in field, then dry in shade for 10-12 days before detopping (leave 2-3 cm neck).
