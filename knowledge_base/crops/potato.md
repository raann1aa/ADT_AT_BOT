# Potato (आलू) Cultivation Guide

## Agro-Climatic Window and Varieties
Potato (*Solanum tuberosum*) is extensively grown in Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal, Bihar, Punjab, Gujarat, and Madhya Pradesh.
- **Sowing Period:**
  - Early Crop: Late September to early October (harvest in 70-75 days).
  - Main Crop: October 15 to November 10 (Varieties: Kufri Jyoti, Kufri Pukhraj, Kufri Bahar, Kufri Chipsona).
- **Soil:** Light sandy loam to loam, rich in organic matter, slightly acidic (pH 5.2 to 6.8). Soil must be loose and friable to allow unhindered tuber expansion.

## Seed Tuber Preparation and Treatment
- **Seed Rate:** 12 to 15 quintals per acre for whole medium tubers (35-45 mm, 40-50 g weight).
- **Sprouting:** Bring seed bags out of cold store 10-14 days before planting; spread in thin layer in diffused daylight to develop short, stout, dark green chits (sprouts).
- **Cut Tubers:** If cutting large tubers, ensure each cut piece has at least 2 viable eyes.
- **Tuber Treatment:** Dip in solution of Mancozeb 75 WP @ 2.5 g/L water or Moncut (Flutolanil) for 10 minutes to prevent black scurf and dry rot; dry in shade before planting.

## Planting Layout and Earthing Up (मिट्टी चढ़ाना)
- **Spacing:** Ridges 60 cm apart, tubers spaced 15 to 20 cm apart on ridges, planted 5-7 cm deep.
- **Earthing Up:**
  - First earthing up: When plants are 15-20 cm tall (25-30 DAS).
  - Second earthing up: At 40-45 DAS along with top dressing.
  - **Why Critical:** Prevents developing tubers from getting exposed to sunlight. Exposed tubers produce **solanine (green skin)** which is bitter, toxic, and unmarketable.

## Fertilizer Schedule (Per Acre)
Heavy potassium feeder:
- **Basal Dose:** 12-15 tonnes FYM, 50 kg DAP, 50 kg MOP (Muriate of Potash), 10 kg Zinc Sulphate.
- **Top Dressing 1 (25 DAS at first earthing up):** 40 kg Urea.
- **Top Dressing 2 (40 DAS at second earthing up):** 30 kg Urea + 25 kg SOP (Sulphate of Potash 00:00:50). Note: SOP gives superior starch content and chip crispness compared to MOP.

## De-haulming (Tops Cutting)
- Cut foliage from ground level 10-12 days before digging.
- Leaves tubers in soil to cure, toughen skin, and reduce harvesting bruising and transit rotting.
