# Rice / Paddy (धान) Cultivation Guide

## Overview and Seasons
Paddy (*Oryza sativa*) is the staple food grain grown extensively during the Kharif season across West Bengal, Uttar Pradesh, Punjab, Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, and Tamil Nadu.
- **Nursery Sowing:** May 20 to June 15.
- **Transplanting:** June 20 to July 20 (when seedlings are 21-25 days old with 4-5 leaves).
- **Direct Seeded Rice (DSR):** Sown in first fortnight of June with moisture using tar-wattar technique.

## Nursery and Seed Rate
- **Transplanted Puddled Rice:** 12 to 15 kg/acre for inbred varieties; 6 to 8 kg/acre for hybrids.
- **System of Rice Intensification (SRI):** 2 kg/acre (single 10-12 day old seedling per hill).
- **Seed Treatment:** Soak seeds in brine solution (salt water) to discard floating unviable seeds. Treat with Carbendazim 50 WP @ 2 g/kg seed or bio-agent *Pseudomonas fluorescens* @ 10 g/kg seed.

## Fertilizer Schedule (Per Acre)
1. **Basal Dose (During Last Puddling):**
   - DAP: 35 kg OR NPK 12:32:16: 50 kg
   - MOP: 20 kg
   - Zinc Sulphate Monohydrate (33%): 7 kg or Heptahydrate (21%): 10 kg
2. **First Top Dressing (Tillering Stage - 21 DAS/DAT):**
   - Neem Coated Urea: 35 kg
3. **Second Top Dressing (Panicle Initiation Stage - 42-45 DAT):**
   - Neem Coated Urea: 30 kg + Potash 10 kg if not applied adequately at basal.

## Water Management
- Maintain a thin water film of 2-3 cm for the first 7-10 days after transplanting to encourage root establishment.
- Maintain 3-5 cm water depth during active tillering and panicle initiation.
- Adopt Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) after tillering to conserve 30% water and reduce methane emissions.
- Drain field completely 10-12 days before harvest for uniform grain ripening.
