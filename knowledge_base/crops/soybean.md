# Soybean (सोयाबीन) Cultivation Guide

## Agro-Climatic Requirements and Sowing
- Premier Kharif oilseed in Madhya Pradesh (Malwa, Bundelkhand), Maharashtra (Vidarbha, Marathwada), and Rajasthan.
- **Sowing Period:** June 20 to July 10 (after receiving at least 75-100 mm of steady monsoon rainfall). Do not sow in dry or under-moist soil.
- **Seed Rate:** 25 to 30 kg per acre for small/medium seeded varieties (JS-335, JS-9560, JS-2034, NRC-127). Ensure seed germination is above 70%.

## Seed Inoculation & Treatment (FIR Method)
1. **Fungicide:** Treat with Carboxin + Thiram @ 2 g/kg seed or Thiram + Carbendazim (2:1).
2. **Insecticide:** Imidacloprid 48 FS @ 1.25 ml/kg or Thiamethoxam 30 FS @ 3 ml/kg to protect seedlings from stem fly and whitefly for 25 days.
3. **Rhizobium & PSB:** Inoculate with *Bradyrhizobium japonicum* culture @ 5 g/kg seed and Phosphorus Solubilizing Bacteria (PSB) @ 5 g/kg seed under shade.

## Land Layout and Broad Bed Furrow (BBF)
- Planting on Broad Bed Furrow (BBF) with 4 rows on a 1.2 m wide bed flanked by 30 cm drainage furrows:
  - Prevents waterlogging during excessive monsoon downpours.
  - Conserves rainwater in furrows during mid-season dry spells (dry spells of 15-20 days).
  - Enhances root aeration and nodule count by 30-40%.

## Fertilizer Schedule (Per Acre)
Soybean fixes atmospheric nitrogen, so basal nitrogen requirement is modest:
- **Basal Dose:** 15 kg Urea, 75 kg Single Super Phosphate (SSP provides both Phosphorus and 11% Sulphur), and 20 kg MOP.
- **Sulphur:** If using DAP instead of SSP, apply 10 kg elemental/bentonite sulphur per acre.
- Avoid top dressing heavy doses of Urea as it stops natural bacterial root nodulation.

## Critical Weed Management
- Critical crop-weed competition period: First 15 to 35 DAS.
- **Pre-emergence herbicide:** Apply Diclosulam 84 WDG @ 12.4 g/acre or Pendimethalin 30 EC @ 1-1.25 L/acre within 48 hours of sowing on moist soil.
- **Post-emergence (15-20 DAS):** Imazethapyr 10 SL @ 400 ml/acre or Propaquizafop 2.5% + Imazethapyr 3.75% ME @ 800 ml/acre.
