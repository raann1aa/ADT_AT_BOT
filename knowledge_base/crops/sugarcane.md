# Sugarcane (गन्ना) Cultivation and Management Guide

## Sowing Seasons and Sett Selection
- **Autumn Planting (Oct - Nov):** Gives 15-20% higher yield and higher sugar recovery.
- **Spring Planting (Feb - March):** Common across UP, Punjab, Haryana, and Bihar.
- **Adsali (July - August):** Grown in Maharashtra and Karnataka (16-18 month duration).
- **Sett Selection:** Use top 1/3rd to 1/2 portion of healthy 8-10 month old cane with sound buds. 3-bud setts require approximately 20,000 to 25,000 setts per acre (35-40 quintals).
- **Sett Treatment:** Dip setts for 10 minutes in Carbendazim 50 WP (100 g in 100 L water) or hot water treatment (52°C for 30 minutes) to eliminate red rot and smut pathogens.

## Planting Methods
- **Trench / Furrow Planting:** Furrows 90 to 120 cm apart. Conserves water, supports mechanical harvesting, and reduces lodging.
- **Ring Pit Method:** Circular pits (90 cm diameter, 30 cm depth) spaced 120 cm apart. Produces up to 60-80 tonnes per acre.

## Fertilizer Schedule (Per Acre)
For plant crop (Target: 40-50 tonnes/acre):
- **Basal Dose:** 50 kg DAP, 50 kg MOP, 10 kg Zinc Sulphate, and 10 tonnes well-decomposed FYM in furrows.
- **First Top Dressing (45 DAS - Tillering):** 45 kg Urea.
- **Second Top Dressing (90 DAS - Grand Growth):** 45 kg Urea.
- **Third Top Dressing (120 DAS - Before monsoon earthing up):** 45 kg Urea + 25 kg MOP.

## Earthing-Up and Propping
- **First Earthing Up:** At 45-60 days with light hoeing.
- **Heavy Earthing Up:** In June-July before monsoon rains to form high ridges, suppress late tillers, and anchor the root zone against cyclone winds.
- **Trash Mulching:** Spread 3-inch layer of dry cane trash in furrows to reduce weed growth and conserve up to 35% soil moisture.
