# Tomato (टमाटर) Cultivation Guide

## Seasons and Seed Rate
- **Sowing Windows:**
  - Kharif: May to June (transplant in June-July)
  - Rabi: September to October (transplant in October-November)
  - Summer: December to January (transplant in January-February)
- **Seed Rate:** 60 to 80 grams per acre for indeterminate hybrids (like Abhinav, Saaho, US-440).
- **Pro-Tray Nursery:** Raise seedlings in 98-cavity pro-trays using sterilized coco-peat mixed with *Trichoderma*. Ready to transplant in 22-25 days.

## Spacing, Mulching, and Trellising (Staking)
- **Bed Preparation:** Raised beds 90 cm wide, 30 cm high, with 45 cm walking furrows.
- **Silver-Black Mulch Film:** 25-30 micron film retains soil moisture, suppresses weeds, and controls thrips/whiteflies by light reflection.
- **Spacing:** 4 to 5 feet bed-to-bed, 1.5 feet plant-to-plant (zigzag on bed).
- **Staking:** Indeterminate varieties must be supported on GI wire trellis with bamboo posts (7-8 feet) and polypropylene twine. Increases marketable yield by 40% and prevents soil-borne fungal rots.

## Fertilizer and Fertigation Schedule (Per Acre)
1. **Basal Dose (In beds before mulch laying):**
   - 8-10 tonnes FYM, 50 kg SSP, 25 kg MOP, 10 kg Micronutrient Grade-II, 25 kg Neem cake.
2. **Fertigation via Drip (Weekly):**
   - **Vegetative Phase (1-30 DAT):** 19:19:19 @ 3 kg per acre twice weekly.
   - **Flowering & Early Fruiting (31-60 DAT):** 12:61:00 (MAP) @ 3 kg + 13:00:45 @ 4 kg per acre.
   - **Harvesting Phase (61 DAT onwards):** 00:00:50 @ 5 kg + Calcium Nitrate @ 3 kg per acre alternate weeks.

## Preventing Blossom End Rot (BER)
- Blossom End Rot (black sunken leathery spot at base of fruit) is caused by **Calcium deficiency** induced by irregular water supply.
- **Remedy:** Ensure steady drip irrigation cycles. Spray Calcium Nitrate @ 3 g/L + Boron (20%) @ 1 g/L at early flowering and fruit set.
