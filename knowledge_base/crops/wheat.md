# Wheat (गेहूं) Cultivation Guide

## Overview and Sowing Window
Wheat (*Triticum aestivum*) is the principal Rabi foodgrain in North and Central India (Punjab, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Bihar).
- **Optimal Sowing Time:** November 1 to November 25 (timely sown); late sown up to December 15-25.
- **Seed Rate:** 40-45 kg per acre for timely sown; 50-55 kg per acre for late sown conditions.
- **Seed Treatment:** Treat seeds with Carboxin + Thiram (Vitavax Power) @ 2.5 g/kg seed or *Trichoderma viride* @ 4 g/kg seed to prevent loose smut and root rot.

## Soil and Land Preparation
- Grows best in well-drained fertile loamy to clay loam soils with a pH between 6.0 and 7.5.
- Perform one deep ploughing followed by 2-3 harrowing operations and planking (pata) to achieve a fine, moisture-retaining seedbed.

## Fertilizer Schedule (Per Acre)
For high-yielding dwarf varieties under irrigated conditions:
1. **Basal Application (At Sowing):**
   - DAP: 50 kg (1 bag) OR NPK 12:32:16: 65 kg
   - MOP (Muriate of Potash): 20 kg
   - Zinc Sulphate (21%): 10 kg (if soil is zinc deficient, do not mix directly with DAP)
2. **First Top Dressing (At CRI Stage - 20 to 25 days after sowing):**
   - Urea: 45 kg (1 bag) right before or after the first irrigation.
3. **Second Top Dressing (At Jointing / Tillering - 40 to 45 days after sowing):**
   - Urea: 45 kg (1 bag) with second irrigation.

## Critical Irrigation Stages
Wheat requires 4 to 6 irrigations depending on soil and winter rainfall. Irrigation at critical growth stages is essential to avoid yield loss:
1. **Crown Root Initiation (CRI) Stage (20-25 DAS):** Most critical! Delay reduces tillering drastically.
2. **Tillering Stage (40-45 DAS):** Promotes strong productive tillers.
3. **Late Jointing Stage (60-65 DAS):** Supports stem elongation.
4. **Flowering / Heading Stage (80-85 DAS):** Critical for spikelet fertility.
5. **Milking Stage (100-105 DAS):** Grain formation stage.
6. **Dough Stage (115-120 DAS):** Prevents premature shriveling. Avoid irrigation on very windy days to prevent lodging (गिरना).
