# Critical Irrigation Stages for Major Indian Crops

When tubewell discharge decreases or canal rotation is delayed, farmers must apply available water strictly at the most yield-sensitive physiological stages:

## Crop-Wise Critical Stages Priority Matrix

| Crop | Most Critical Stage (Priority 1) | Secondary Critical Stage (Priority 2) | Tertiary Stage (Priority 3) | Stages When Water Can Be Withheld |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Wheat (गेहूं)** | **CRI Stage (20-25 DAS)** (Crown root initiation) | **Heading / Flowering (80-85 DAS)** | **Late Jointing & Milking (45 & 100 DAS)** | Late dough stage near maturity |
| **Paddy / Rice (धान)** | **Panicle Initiation to Flowering** | **Active Tillering (20-35 DAT)** | **Grain Filling / Milk** | 10 days before harvesting (Drain dry) |
| **Maize (मक्का)** | **Tasseling & Silking (45-60 DAS)** (Moisture stress causes barren cobs) | **Early Blister / Grain Filling (70-75 DAS)** | **Knee-High Stage (25-30 DAS)** | Early seedling & dough stage |
| **Gram / Chana (चना)** | **Branching / Pre-Flowering (40-45 DAS)** | **Pod Filling Stage (65-75 DAS)** | - | **NEVER irrigate during peak flowering (causes flower drop!)** |
| **Mustard (सरसों)** | **Pre-flowering / Rosette (30-35 DAS)** | **Siliqua / Pod Development (55-65 DAS)** | - | Early seedling & grain ripening |
| **Cotton (कपास)** | **Peak Flowering (60-70 DAS)** | **Boll Development (80-100 DAS)** | **Square Formation (40-45 DAS)** | Vegetative phase (forces deep root growth) |
| **Soybean (सोयाबीन)** | **Pod Filling / Grain Development (65-75 DAS)** | **Flowering (40-45 DAS)** | - | Early vegetative (BBF drainage priority) |

## 3 Rules of Smart Water Management
1. **Never Let Soil Dry to Wilting Point During Flowering/Pollination:** Moisture stress at pollination causes pollen sterility and abortion of flowers across all crops.
2. **Irrigate in Early Morning or Late Evening:** Mid-day flood irrigation in hot weather causes boiling of root zone water, damaging root hairs and evaporating 30-40% of applied water.
3. **Use Mulching in Inter-Row Spaces:** In sugarcane, cotton, and orchards, covering bare soil with dry crop residue or organic trash reduces surface evaporation by up to 40%.
