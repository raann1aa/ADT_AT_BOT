# Drip Irrigation System Maintenance & Acid Treatment

## 1. Routine Filter Maintenance (Weekly)
Filter clogging is the leading cause of uneven water distribution and low operating pressure:
- **Screen & Disc Filters:**
  - Check pressure differential gauge between inlet and outlet. If differential exceeds 0.5 kg/cm² (7 psi), filter is choked.
  - Open filter housing, remove disc rings/mesh, wash under high-pressure water with a soft nylon brush, and reassemble. Never scrub with wire brushes which tear the fine screen.
- **Hydrocyclone / Sand Media Filters:**
  - Backwash weekly for 5-7 minutes by reversing flow until discharge water runs crystal clear.

---

## 2. Flushing Sub-mains and Lateral Lines (Every 15 Days)
- Open flush valves at ends of sub-mains one by one while pump runs at full pressure. Run for 2-3 minutes until clear water discharges.
- Open the end plugs (figure-of-8 ends) of lateral drip lines in small groups (5-10 lines at a time). Let water rush through to eject accumulated sand and silt particles.

---

## 3. Acid Treatment for Clogged Drippers (एसिड ट्रीटमेंट)
In areas with hard, saline, or high-calcium groundwater (bicarbonates and carbonates), drippers gradually clog with white chalky scale. **Hydrochloric Acid (Commercial Muriatic Acid 30-33%)** dissolves scale without damaging polyethylene lateral pipes:

### Acid Treatment Procedure
1. **Safety First:** Wear rubber gloves, eye goggles, and plastic boots. Always add acid to water, NEVER water to acid!
2. **Calculate Acid Quantity:** Dilute commercial Hydrochloric Acid (HCl 33%) with water in a plastic bucket to achieve a 0.6% acid concentration (pH ~ 2.0 to 3.0 at dripper discharge). Typically 1.5 to 2.0 liters of commercial HCl per 1000 liters of system volume.
3. **Injection via Venturi:** Run pump at normal operating pressure. Inject diluted acid through the Venturi injector into the drip system.
4. **Monitoring:** Test water dripping from the furthest end of laterals with litmus paper or pH meter. When pH drops to 2.5-3.0 (litmus turns red), shut down the pump immediately!
5. **Soaking Period:** Leave the acid solution inside the closed drip lines for **12 to 24 hours**. The acid completely dissolves calcium and magnesium carbonate scale.
6. **Thorough Flushing:** Next morning, open all lateral end plugs and run fresh clean water for 30 minutes to flush out all dissolved residue.

---

## 4. Chlorine (Bleach) Treatment for Algae & Bacterial Slime
If groundwater contains iron bacteria, sulfur slime, or organic algae from farm ponds:
- Inject Sodium Hypochlorite (household bleach 10-12% available chlorine) to maintain 10-20 ppm free residual chlorine at lateral ends.
- Soak for 4 hours, then flush thoroughly.
- *Caution:* Never mix chlorine and acid in the same tank simultaneously, as it produces lethal chlorine gas! Perform treatments on separate days.
