# Crop Protection Against Extreme Weather: Frost & Heatwaves

## 1. Protecting Crops from Winter Frost (पाला / तुषार)
- **Occurs:** Late December to mid-January when clear night sky, calm winds, and temperatures drop below 4°C. Water inside plant vascular cells freezes, expands, and bursts cell walls. Leaves turn blackened and papery.
- **Most Sensitive Crops:** Potato, Tomato, Mustard, Gram, Brinjal, and young fruit orchards. (Wheat is relatively tolerant).

### Proven Emergency Field Actions
1. **Light Evening Irrigation:** Moist soil has high specific heat capacity and conducts geothermal heat to the surface during freezing nights. Run light sprinkler or furrow irrigation on frost-threatened evenings.
2. **Smoke Screens (धुआं करना):** Burn dried crop weeds, cow dung cakes, and moist straw on the North-West field bunds (direction of cold winds) between 11 PM and 5 AM. Smoke forms a protective canopy that reduces radiative heat loss by 2°C to 3°C.
3. **Chemical / Sulphur Sprays (Before Frost Strikes):**
   - Foliar spray of **Water Soluble Sulphur (80% WDG) @ 2 to 3 g/L** OR
   - Spray **0.1% Dilute Sulphuric Acid** (1 ml concentrated commercial H2SO4 per liter of water). Dissolves cell sap freezing threshold and protects foliage for 10-14 days.
   - Spray **Thiophanate Methyl 70 WP @ 1 g/L** to prevent secondary fungal entry through ruptured cell walls.

## 2. Protecting Wheat and Crops from Terminal Heatwaves (मार्च की लू / समय पूर्व गर्मी)
- **Problem:** Sudden temperature spikes in late February and early March (temperatures rising above 32-35°C during heading/milking) cause premature desiccation and shriveling of wheat grains, cutting yields by 15-25%.

### Mitigation Practices
1. **Foliar Spray of Potassium Nitrate (13:00:45):**
   - Spray 13:00:45 @ 10 g per liter of water (2 kg per 200 L water per acre) at Booting stage (early heading) and repeat at 50% Anthesis/Flowering.
   - Potassium regulates stomatal transpiration and maintains cellular turgor, mitigating heat shock.
2. **Micro-Sprinkler Cooling:**
   - Run portable sprinklers for 25-30 minutes during peak hot afternoon hours (12 PM to 2 PM) to lower microclimate canopy temperature by 3°C to 4°C.
3. **Spray Salicylic Acid:**
   - Foliar spray of Salicylic Acid @ 15 ppm (15 mg per liter of water) induces plant systemic acquired resistance (SAR) against thermal stress.
