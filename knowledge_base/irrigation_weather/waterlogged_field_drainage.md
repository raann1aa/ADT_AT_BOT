# Waterlogged Field Drainage & Post-Flood Crop Rescue

## 1. Dangers of Waterlogging (जलभराव के खतरे)
Upland Kharif crops (Cotton, Maize, Soybean, Pulses, Tomato, Chilli) are extremely sensitive to standing water:
- **Within 24 to 48 Hours of Standing Water:** Soil pores become completely saturated, displacing oxygen. Plant roots suffocate (hypoxia), aerobic respiration stops, and roots fail to take up nitrogen and phosphorus.
- **Signs:** Leaves turn limp, yellow, and drop. Stems become spongy and rot at the soil line (collar rot/damping off). Secondary fungal infections (*Pythium*, *Phytophthora*, *Rhizoctonia*) destroy the root cortex.

---

## 2. Emergency Water Evacuation (Immediate Step)
1. **Cut Surface Drainage Trenches:** Cut peripheral boundary trenches and cross-furrows at the lowest elevation corners of the field to evacuate standing water into main village drainage nalas within 12 to 24 hours.
2. **Clear Silt Crust:** Once surface water recedes, do not let muddy silt dry into a sun-baked cement-like crust. As soon as the soil is somewhat walkable, use a light hand-hoe or wheel-hoe (khurpi/kudali) to break the top crust. This restores soil aeration and lets oxygen reach the asphyxiated root zone.

---

## 3. Crop Revival Protocol (Once Water Recedes)
Roots are temporarily non-functional, so **soil-applied granular fertilizers will NOT work and will be wasted!** Rely on emergency foliar feeding:

### Step 1: Emergency Foliar Feeding (To Restore Green Color)
- Foliar spray of **2% Urea (20 g per liter of water = 4 kg in 200 L water/acre)** OR
- **19:19:19 @ 5 g/L + Micronutrient Grade-II @ 2 g/L**.
- Urea absorbed through stomata bypasses paralyzed roots and restores photosynthetic chlorophyll synthesis within 48-72 hours.

### Step 2: Anti-Root-Rot Fungicide Spray / Drenching
Waterlogged soils breed virulent soil-borne fungal pathogens:
- Drench root collars or spray base of stems with:
  - **Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP (Ridomil Gold) @ 2.5 g per liter of water** OR
  - **Copper Oxychloride 50% WP (Blitox) @ 3 g per liter of water**.
- If farming organically, drench root zone with *Trichoderma viride* (10 g/L) enriched in buttermilk.

### Step 3: Root Stimulation
- Foliar spray of **Humic Acid (12% liquid) @ 3 ml/L** stimulates rapid generation of new white feeder roots once aeration is restored.
