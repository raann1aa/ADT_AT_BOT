# Sucking Pests: Aphids, Jassids & Whiteflies Management

## Pest Overview & Damage Mechanism
Sucking insects suck cell sap from tender shoots, buds, and under-surfaces of leaves:
1. **Aphids (माहो / चेपा / लही):** Soft, tiny green or black insects clustering on tender stems and buds (common on mustard, wheat, potato).
2. **Jassids / Leafhoppers (हरा तेला):** Wedge-shaped yellowish-green insects walking diagonally; inject toxins causing "hopper burn" (leaf margins turn yellow, curl down, and scorch).
3. **Whiteflies (सफेद मक्खी):** Tiny white moth-like insects flitting under leaves. Transmit devastating viral diseases (Cotton Leaf Curl Virus, Tomato Leaf Curl Virus, Soybean Yellow Mosaic Virus).

## General Damage Symptoms
- Yellowing, curling, and crinkling of leaves.
- Secretion of clear, sticky liquid called **honeydew (मधु जैसा चिपचिपा पदार्थ)**.
- Growth of black fungal coating called **sooty mold (काली फफूंद)** on leaves, which blocks photosynthesis.

## Non-Chemical Cultural & Mechanical Measures
- **Yellow & Blue Sticky Traps:** Install 15-20 yellow sticky traps per acre at 1 foot above the crop canopy to trap whiteflies, winged aphids, and leafminers. (Use blue traps for thrips).
- **Barrier Crops:** Grow 2-3 border rows of tall pearl millet (bajra), maize, or sorghum around cotton/chilli fields to filter incoming whiteflies.
- **Reflective Mulch:** Silver-black plastic mulch repels incoming flying vectors through ultraviolet reflection.
- **Neem Spray:** Spray Cold-pressed Neem Oil (10,000 ppm) @ 2-3 ml/liter + 1 ml liquid soap every 10 days as a repellent and antifeedant.

## Targeted Chemical Interventions

### For Aphids (Mustard, Wheat, Vegetables)
- **Dimethoate 30% EC (Rogor):** 300 ml/acre (1.5 ml/L) OR
- **Thiamethoxam 25% WG (Actara):** 80-100 g/acre (0.5 g/L) OR
- **Imidacloprid 17.8% SL (Confidor):** 60-80 ml/acre (0.4 ml/L).

### For Whitefly (Cotton, Chilli, Tomato, Pulses)
- **Egg / Nymph Stage:**
  - Pyriproxyfen 10% EC (Lano) @ 400-500 ml/acre (insect growth regulator that prevents nymph molting) OR
  - Diafenthiuron 50% WP (Pegasus) @ 250 g/acre.
- **Adult Whitefly Outbreak:**
  - Spiromesifen 22.9% SC (Oberon) @ 200 ml/acre OR
  - Afidopyropen 50 g/L DC (Sefina) @ 400 ml/acre OR
  - Flonicamid 50% WG (Ulala) @ 60-80 g/acre (highly safe for beneficial ladybird beetles and honeybees).

*Note: Alternate chemical classes to prevent the rapid development of insecticide resistance.*
