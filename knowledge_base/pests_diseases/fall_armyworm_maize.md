# Fall Armyworm (FAW) in Maize Control

## Pest Profile & Damage Symptoms
- **Pest:** *Spodoptera frugiperda* (invasive moth pest attacking maize, sorghum, and millets).
- **Early Symptoms (Seedling Stage):** Young larvae scrape the green epidermal tissue of tender leaves, creating papery, transparent **window-pane patches**.
- **Whorl Damage (Knee-high Stage):** Older caterpillars enter deep inside the central leaf whorl. They chew large irregular holes in expanding leaves and produce heavy, moist, **sawdust-like yellowish-brown fecal pellets (frass)** that fill the leaf funnel.
- **Tassel and Cob Damage:** Bore into young tassels and enter the cob from the top, eating kernels and opening paths for fungal cob rot.

## Identification of the Caterpillar
Look for two distinct field markers on the mature caterpillar:
1. An inverted white **"Y" mark** on the front of its dark head.
2. **Four elevated black spots arranged in a perfect square** on the eighth abdominal segment near the tail end.

## Control Measures

### 1. Mechanical & Eco-friendly (Early Stages)
- Hand-pick egg masses (covered in golden-brown scales) and young larvae in small holdings.
- Drop dry sand or soil mixed with wood ash (9:1 ratio) directly into the central whorls of maize plants; abrasives dehydrate and kill young caterpillars inside the whorl.
- Install FAW pheromone traps @ 5 traps/acre for adult moth monitoring.

### 2. Biological Control
- Release *Trichogramma pretiosum* or *Telenomus remus* egg parasitoids @ 50,000/acre.
- Spray *Metarhizium rileyi* (formerly *Nomuraea rileyi*) formulation @ 3 g/L or *Bacillus thuringiensis* (Bt var. kurstaki) @ 2 g/L during late evening hours.

### 3. Targeted Chemical Insecticide Application
Direct the spray nozzle straight down into the **central whorl (funnel)** of each plant using a knapsack sprayer:
- **First Window (15-20 DAS, early instars):**
  - Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 80 g per acre (0.4 g/L water) OR
  - Thiamethoxam 12.6% + Lambda-cyhalothrin 9.5% ZC @ 80 ml per acre.
- **Second Window (30-40 DAS, heavy infestation):**
  - Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC (Coragen) @ 80 ml per acre (0.4 ml/L water) OR
  - Spinetoram 11.7% SC (Delegate) @ 100 ml per acre.
- **Poison Bait Method (For Large Caterpillars):** Mix 10 kg rice bran + 2 kg jaggery in 3-4 liters water, ferment for 24 hours, then mix 100 g Thiodicarb 75 WP or 100 ml Chlorpyrifos 20 EC. Roll into small pellets and drop into infested plant whorls.
