# Late Blight of Potato & Tomato (पछेती झुलसा)

## Causal Agent & Weather Trigger
- **Pathogen:** *Phytophthora infestans* (oomycete water mold).
- **Trigger Weather:** Cool nights (10-15°C), moderate days (18-22°C), high relative humidity (>90%), continuous fog, rain, or overcast dewy days in December and January. It can destroy an entire field in 4 to 7 days if uncontrolled.

## Diagnostic Field Symptoms
1. **Leaves:** Small, water-soaked, irregular pale-to-dark green spots appear at tips and leaf margins. Spots rapidly enlarge into brown-black necrotic lesions.
2. **Underside White Mildew:** In early morning dew or high humidity, a **delicate white cottony fungal growth** appears on the margins of spots on the underside of leaves.
3. **Stems:** Dark brown to black lesions girdle stems and petioles, causing the whole vine to collapse.
4. **Tubers / Fruit:**
   - Potato: Irregular purplish-brown dry depressed rot under the skin that turns into firm reddish-brown granular rot inside.
   - Tomato: Large, greasy brown marbling on green fruit with firm surface.

## Preventive Management (Before Blight Strikes)
When cloudy/foggy weather is forecast, spray protective contact fungicides:
- **Mancozeb 75% WP (Dithane M-45):** 600-800 g per acre (2.5 g/L water) OR
- **Propineb 70% WP (Antracol):** 600 g per acre (2.5 g/L water) OR
- **Chlorothalonil 75% WP (Kavach):** 400 g per acre.
Repeat every 7-10 days if foggy conditions continue.

## Curative Chemical Control (When Spots Appear in Field)
Switch immediately to systemic translaminar fungicides:
1. **Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP (Ridomil Gold):**
   - **Dose:** 500 g per acre (2.5 g/liter of water).
2. **Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP (Curzate / Sectin):**
   - **Dose:** 600 g per acre (3 g/liter of water).
3. **Dimethomorph 50% WP (Acrobat):**
   - **Dose:** 200 g per acre (1 g/L) mixed with Mancozeb 400 g.
4. **Mandipropamid 23.4% SC (Revus):**
   - **Dose:** 160 ml per acre (0.8 ml/L).

**Crucial Advice for Potato Farmers:**
- If late blight attacks late in the season, immediately cut and remove all green vines (**de-haulming**) and spray ridges with Copper Oxychloride. Do NOT dig tubers while green blighted leaves are touching the ground, as spores wash into the soil and rot tubers in storage.
