# Organic Pest & Disease Management Guide

## 1. Neem Formulations (नीम आधारित कीटनाशक)
Neem contains active azadirachtin, which acts as a repellent, antifeedant, oviposition deterrent, and insect growth regulator (IGR). It does not kill beneficial insects like bees and spider predators.
- **Neem Oil 10,000 PPM / 1500 PPM:**
  - Dose: 3 to 5 ml per liter of water.
  - Preparation: Mix neem oil with 1 ml of mild liquid soap or washing powder in a cup of water, stir vigorously until milky emulsion forms, then mix into sprayer water.
- **Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE 5% - नीम की गिरी का अर्क):**
  - Grind 5 kg dried neem seeds into fine powder.
  - Soak in 10 liters of water overnight; stir with a wooden stick.
  - Squeeze through a muslin cloth, filter, and dilute to 100 liters of water. Add 100 g soap powder before spraying.

## 2. Beneficial Entomopathogenic Fungi (जैविक कीटनाशक)
Live microbial agents that infect and kill pest bodies:
- **Beauveria bassiana:**
  - Highly effective against caterpillars, bollworms, borer larvae, and grubs.
  - Dose: 5 g powder or 5 ml liquid formulation per liter of water. Spray in late evening hours (UV sunlight deactivates spores).
- **Verticillium lecanii:**
  - Specific for sucking pests like whiteflies, mealybugs, and aphids.
  - Infects pests in high relative humidity (>80%).
- **Metarhizium anisopliae:**
  - Targets underground root grubs (white grubs), termites, and cutworms. Apply 2-3 kg/acre mixed with 100 kg moist FYM around the root zone.

---

## 3. Bio-Fungicides & Antagonists
- **Trichoderma viride / Trichoderma harzianum:**
  - Controls soil-borne pathogens: *Fusarium* (wilt), *Rhizoctonia* (root rot), *Pythium* (damping off), and *Sclerotium*.
  - **Seed Treatment:** 4-5 g per kg seed.
  - **Soil Enrichment:** Mix 2 kg Trichoderma powder with 100 kg well-decomposed FYM, cover with moist gunny bags under shade for 7-10 days until green fungal mycelium multiplies, then broadcast in 1 acre during final ploughing.
- **Pseudomonas fluorescens:**
  - Controls bacterial wilt, leaf spots, and sheath blight in paddy and vegetables.

## 4. Dashparni Ark (दसपर्णी अर्क - 10 Leaf Botanical Spray)
A traditional formulation repellent to chewing caterpillars and sucking pests:
- **Ingredients:** Leaves of 10 non-edible/bitter trees (Neem, Calotropis/Aak, Custard Apple/Sitaphal, Papaya, Lantana/Ghaneri, Castor/Arandi, Guava, Karanj, Datura, Nirgundi) - 2 kg of each.
- Add 5 liters cow urine (Gaumutra), 2 kg fresh cow dung, 500 g crushed ginger, 500 g garlic paste, 500 g hot green chilli paste.
- Ferment in a plastic drum in shade for 30-40 days. Stir clockwise daily.
- **Application:** Filter and use 200 to 250 ml of Dashparni Ark per 15-liter knapsack sprayer.
