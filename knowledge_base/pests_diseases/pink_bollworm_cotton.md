# Pink Bollworm (गुलाबी सुंडी) in Cotton Management

## Identification and Life Cycle
- **Pest:** *Pectinophora gossypiella* (Lepidoptera).
- Feeds inside squares, flowers, and developing bolls. Because larvae bore into green bolls within 30-40 minutes of hatching and seal the entry hole, foliar sprays cannot reach them once inside.

## Diagnostic Field Signs
1. **Rosette Flower (गुलाबनुमा फूल):** Larva weaves petals together with silk threads. The flower fails to open normally and looks like an unopened rosebud.
2. **Double Seed / Stained Lint:** Inside mature bolls, larvae feed on developing seeds and stain the white lint dirty brown/yellow.
3. **Premature Boll Opening:** Infested bolls open prematurely with rotten, poor-quality fiber.

## Integrated Pest Management (IPM) Strategy

### 1. Pheromone Trap Monitoring (45 DAS onwards)
- Install **Pheromone Traps** with *Gossyplure* lures @ 4 to 5 traps per acre.
- **Economic Threshold Level (ETL):**
  - Continuous catch of 8 male moths per trap for 3 consecutive nights OR
  - 10% damaged green bolls with live larvae.

### 2. Cultural & Mechanical Control
- Hand-pick and destroy all rosette flowers daily during early flowering.
- Terminate the cotton crop by December or early January; avoid extending the crop to February/March (no ratoon cotton) to break the pest life cycle.
- Graze cattle or shred crop stalks after final picking.

### 3. Biological Control
- Release egg parasitoid *Trichogramma bactrae* @ 60,000 parasitized eggs/acre/week starting at 45 DAS (4-5 releases).
- Spray Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE 5%) or Azadirachtin 1500 ppm @ 5 ml/L at first flowering as an oviposition deterrent.

### 4. Chemical Control (Targeting Egg Hatching & Young Larvae)
Spray strictly during the egg-hatching or early boll stage before larvae enter bolls:
- **At 60-75 DAS (Square & Early Boll):**
  - Profenofos 50% EC @ 400 ml/acre (2 ml/L) OR Chlorpyrifos 20% EC @ 500 ml/acre.
- **At 75-100 DAS (Peak Boll Formation):**
  - Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 100 g/acre (0.5 g/L) OR Spinetoram 11.7% SC @ 170 ml/acre.
- **At 100+ DAS (Heavy Infestation):**
  - Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC (Coragen) @ 60 ml/acre OR Flubendiamide 39.35% SC (Fame) @ 50 ml/acre.
