# Onion Purple Blotch & Storage Rot (प्याज की सड़न) Management

## 1. Purple Blotch in Field (बैंगनी धब्बा रोग)
- **Pathogen:** *Alternaria porri* (fungus).
- **Symptoms:**
  - Water-soaked small spots appear on leaves and seed scapes.
  - Spots rapidly turn into dark purple/violet centers surrounded by concentric yellowish rings.
  - In advanced attacks, leaf tops collapse at the lesion point, depriving the developing bulb of photosynthates and reducing bulb size by 25-50%.

### Field Fungicide Schedule
- Avoid overhead sprinkler irrigation in the late evening, which keeps leaves wet overnight.
- Always add a sticker / wetting agent (Silicon spreader @ 0.5 ml/L) because onion leaves have a waxy, slippery cuticle.
- **First Protective Spray:** Mancozeb 75 WP @ 2.5 g/L OR Propineb 70 WP @ 2.5 g/L.
- **Curative Spray (On active purple spots):**
  - **Tebuconazole 50% + Trifloxystrobin 25% WG (Nativo):** 100-120 g per acre (0.5-0.6 g/L) OR
  - **Difenoconazole 25% EC (Score):** 150-200 ml per acre (1 ml/L) OR
  - **Azoxystrobin 11% + Tebuconazole 18.3% SC (Custodia):** 250-300 ml per acre.

## 2. Storage Rot in Warehouses (प्याज भंडारण में सड़न)
Post-harvest losses in traditional storage structures (Chawl / Kanda Chawl) reach 30-40% due to black mold, soft rot, and sprouting:

### Root Causes
- Harvesting immature bulbs or unseasonable rainfall right before digging.
- Over-fertilization with Nitrogen/Urea in the final month before harvest (creates soft, watery, thick neck tissues).
- Poor field curing and cutting neck too close to the bulb.

### 5-Point Protocol for Rot-Free Onion Storage
1. **Withhold Irrigation:** Stop watering strictly 10 to 15 days before harvesting.
2. **Neck Fall Timing:** Dig bulbs when 50% tops have naturally lodged (50% neck fall).
3. **Double Curing:**
   - 3-5 days in field covered with foliage.
   - 10-12 days under shaded, well-ventilated sheds.
4. **Neck Cutting Rule:** Leave at least **2.5 to 3.5 cm (1 to 1.5 inch)** of dry stalk attached to the top. Cutting too close leaves an open wound through which fungi and bacteria penetrate straight into the core scales.
5. **Pre-Harvest Spray:** 10-15 days before digging, spray Carbendazim 50 WP @ 1 g/L or Mancozeb @ 2.5 g/L.
6. **Storage Structure:** Stack bulbs no more than 4 to 5 feet high in well-ventilated, elevated bamboo/wire-mesh racks with side airflow. Sort out doubles, rots, and thick-necked bulbs before storing.
