# Rice Yellow Stem Borer & Blast Disease Management

## 1. Yellow Stem Borer (पीला तना छेदक)
- **Pest:** *Scirpophaga incertulas*. Larva bores into the central stem and cuts off vascular supply.

### Diagnostic Symptoms
- **Dead Heart (सफेद गोभ / मृत केंद्र):** During tillering stage, the central leaf whorl dries up, turns yellow/brown, and dies. If pulled, the dead shoot slips out easily from the base and emits a foul odor.
- **White Earhead (सफेद बालियां):** During panicle emergence, entire panicles turn chalky white and remain empty without grain (chaffy).

### Control Measures
- Clip seedling leaf tips before transplanting to destroy egg masses laid near the tips.
- Install pheromone traps with *Scirpolure* @ 5 traps/acre.
- **Granular Application (At 20-25 DAT with light standing water):**
  - Chlorantraniliprole 0.4% GR (Ferterra) @ 4 kg per acre OR
  - Cartap Hydrochloride 4% G (Caldan 4G) @ 7.5 kg per acre OR
  - Fipronil 0.3% GR @ 8 kg per acre.
  - *Keep water standing in the field for 48-72 hours after applying granules.*
- **Foliar Spray Alternative:**
  - Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 60 ml/acre OR
  - Flubendiamide 20% WG @ 50 g/acre in 200 L water.

## 2. Rice Blast Disease (धान का ब्लास्ट रोग)
- **Pathogen:** *Magnaporthe oryzae* (fungus). Attacked by excessive nitrogen fertilizer, high humidity (>90%), and cool nights (20°C).

### Symptoms by Plant Stage
- **Leaf Blast:** Spindle/eye-shaped spots with grey/ash-colored center and dark brown-to-reddish margins. Spots coalesce and dry up entire leaves.
- **Node Blast:** Blackening and rotting of stem nodes; stems snap easily at the node.
- **Neck Blast (गर्दन तोड़ ब्लास्ट):** The neck of the panicle rots, turns black/brown, and breaks over. Grains become completely unfilled.

### Control Measures
- Avoid overdosing nitrogen/urea; apply balanced potash.
- **Seed Treatment:** Carbendazim 50 WP @ 2 g/kg seed or Tricyclazole 75 WP @ 2 g/kg.
- **Foliar Spray (At first appearance of leaf spots or booting stage):**
  - **Tricyclazole 75% WP (Baan / Beam):** 120-150 g per acre (standard specialist blast fungicide).
  - **Kasugamycin 3% SL:** 400-500 ml per acre.
  - **Isoprothiolane 40% EC (Fuji-One):** 300-350 ml per acre.
  - **Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC:** 200 ml per acre.
