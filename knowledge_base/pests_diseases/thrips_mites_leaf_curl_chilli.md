# Chilli Leaf Curl (मुरडिया / चुरड़ा-मुरड़ा) Diagnosis & Remedy

## Critical Diagnostic Rule: Thrips vs. Mites vs. Virus
Farmers often confuse three distinct causes of leaf curl in chilli. Look closely at the leaf curl direction:

| Symptom | Cause | Vector / Organism |
|---|---|---|
| **Upward Curling (Boat-shaped / कटोरीनुमा ऊपर की ओर)** | **Thrips (थ्रिप्स)** | *Scirtothrips dorsalis* |
| **Downward Curling (Inverted spoon / उल्टी चम्मच जैसी नीचे की ओर)** | **Yellow Mites (माइट्स / मकड़ी)** | *Polyphagotarsonemus latus* |
| **Mosaic Yellowing + Stunted Bushy Leaves** | **Chilli Leaf Curl Virus (Gemini virus)** | Transmitted by Whitefly |

---

## 1. Upward Leaf Curl: Thrips Management
- **Signs:** Leaves curl upward, margins crinkle, undersides show silvery or bronze scratch marks. Buds and flowers drop.
- **Remedies:**
  - Install **Blue Sticky Traps** @ 20-25 per acre (thrips are attracted to blue wavelength).
  - Spray **Fipronil 5% SC (Regent):** 350-400 ml per acre (2 ml/L) OR
  - **Spinetoram 11.7% SC (Delegate):** 180 ml per acre (0.9 ml/L) OR
  - **Tolfenpyrad 15% EC (Kephyne):** 300 ml per acre.

---

## 2. Downward Leaf Curl: Mite Management
- **Signs:** Leaves become brittle, leathery, dark green, and curl downwards like an inverted spoon or umbrella ribs. Elongated petioles ("rat-tail" appearance).
- **Remedies:**
  - Standard insecticides (like synthetic pyrethroids) actually worsen mite populations by killing predator mites!
  - Spray specific acaricides / miticides:
    - **Spiromesifen 22.9% SC (Oberon):** 200 ml per acre (1 ml/L) OR
    - **Fenpyroximate 5% EC (Sedna):** 300 ml per acre (1.5 ml/L) OR
    - **Propargite 57% EC (Omite):** 400 ml per acre (2 ml/L) OR
    - **Water-soluble Sulphur 80% WDG:** 600 g per acre (3 g/L).

---

## 3. Viral Leaf Curl (Virus Infection)
- Once a plant is systemically infected with leaf curl virus, no chemical can cure the internal virus.
- **Action:** Rogue out (uproot) and bury severely stunted viral plants immediately to prevent whiteflies from spreading it to healthy plants.
- Control whitefly vectors using Diafenthiuron 50 WP (250 g/acre) or Flonicamid 50 WG (60 g/acre).
- Spray Micronutrient mixture + Amino acid stimulant to help unaffected side shoots flush out healthy leaves.
