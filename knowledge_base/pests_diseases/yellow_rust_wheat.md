# Yellow Rust / Stripe Rust (पीला रतुआ) in Wheat

## Causal Organism & Favorable Weather
- **Pathogen:** *Puccinia striiformis f. sp. tritici* (fungus).
- **Favorable Weather:** Humid, cloudy weather with cool temperatures (10°C to 20°C), persistent fog, morning dew, and light drizzle in northern plains and sub-mountainous zones (Punjab, Haryana, Terai, HP, J&K).

## Symptoms & Identification
1. **Yellow Powder Test:** Bright yellow pustules appear arranged in straight, parallel stripes along the leaf veins on the upper leaf surface.
2. When rubbing infected leaves with your fingers, a **fine yellow powder (fungal urediniospores)** rubs off onto your skin or clothes.
3. In advanced stages, yellow stripes turn blackish (telia stage) and entire leaves turn necrotic, drying up prematurely.
4. Severely infected plants produce shriveled, light grains, causing 30% to 70% yield loss if not treated before heading.

## Preventive Cultural Practices
- Sow rust-resistant certified varieties recommended for the zone: DBW 187, DBW 222, DBW 303, HD 3086, HD 3226, PBW 725.
- Avoid late sowing in moisture-prone river beds or foothills.
- Monitor fields regularly, especially shaded areas under trees or water channels where rust spots first appear (focus patches).

## Chemical Control (Treatment Upon First Appearance)
Immediately upon noticing the first yellow pustules in the field, spray any of the following systemic fungicides with 200 liters of water per acre:
1. **Propiconazole 25% EC (Tilt / Bumper):**
   - **Dose:** 200 ml per acre (1 ml per liter of water).
   - Most proven, standard systemic fungicide against yellow and brown rust.
2. **Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC (Amistar Top):**
   - **Dose:** 200 ml per acre (1 ml per liter of water).
3. **Tebuconazole 25.9% EC (Folicur):**
   - **Dose:** 200 ml per acre.

**Spray Guidelines:**
- Use a hollow cone nozzle and ensure complete leaf coverage, particularly the flag leaf.
- If cloudy/dewy weather persists for another 15 days, repeat spray with an alternate chemical.
