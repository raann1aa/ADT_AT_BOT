# Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Scheme Guide

## 1. Purpose & Scope of KCC
The Kisan Credit Card (KCC) scheme provides adequate and timely credit support from the banking system to farmers for:
1. Meeting short-term credit requirements for cultivating crops (seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, diesel, labor).
2. Post-harvest expenses and produce marketing loans.
3. Maintenance of farm assets, pump sets, tractors, and livestock/dairy/fisheries.

---

## 2. Interest Rate & Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI)
- **Base Interest Rate:** 7.0% per annum on short-term crop loans up to **Rs. 3.00 Lakhs**.
- **Interest Subvention Scheme (ISS):** The Central Government provides a 2% interest subvention to commercial banks and cooperative banks.
- **Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI):** An additional **3.0% interest subvention** is awarded to farmers who repay their crop loan dues on or before the due date.
- **Net Effective Interest Rate:** **Only 4.0% per annum!** (7% base minus 3% prompt incentive).
- If the farmer defaults on repayment date, interest reverts to standard commercial card rate (7% to 11%).

---

## 3. Collateral-Free Limit & Scale of Finance
- **Collateral-Free Loan Limit:** **Rs. 1.60 Lakhs** without any pledge of land documents or hypothecation of assets. (Can be enhanced up to Rs. 3.00 Lakhs under tie-up arrangements).
- **Loan Amount Calculation (Scale of Finance):**
  - Determined by District Level Technical Committee (DLTC) based on crop and acreage.
  - Formula: (Cultivated Area × Scale of Finance per acre for the crop) + 10% for post-harvest/household consumption + 20% for farm asset maintenance.
  - A built-in annual limit increment of 10% is sanctioned for up to 5 years (validity of KCC card is 5 years).

---

## 4. Extension to Animal Husbandry & Fisheries
- KCC facility is also available for Dairy, Poultry, Sheep/Goat rearing, and Fisheries:
- Short-term working capital limit of up to **Rs. 2.00 Lakhs** at the same concessional 4% effective interest rate (within the overall ceiling of Rs. 3 Lakhs).

---

## 5. How to Apply & Required Documents
- **Eligibility:** All farmers (individual or joint cultivators, tenant farmers, oral lessees, sharecroppers, and Self Help Groups).
- **Documents Required:**
  1. Simplified one-page KCC application form.
  2. Identity & Address proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID).
  3. Land Ownership Document (Khasra/Khatauni/7/12 extract) certified by Revenue authority.
  4. Affidavit/declaration of crops sown and area cultivated.
  5. Passport size photograph.
- **Processing Time:** Banks are mandated to process and issue KCC within **14 days** of receiving a completed application.
