# Per Drop More Crop (Micro-Irrigation) Scheme

## 1. Scheme Objectives & Benefits
Under Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY - Per Drop More Crop), the government promotes micro-irrigation technologies (Drip & Sprinkler):
- **Water Saving:** Saves 40% to 60% irrigation water compared to conventional flood irrigation.
- **Fertilizer Saving:** Allows direct fertigation through drip lines, cutting fertilizer usage by 25-30% while increasing crop yield by 20% to 45%.
- **Energy & Labor Saving:** Lowers electricity pumping costs and weeding labor.

---

## 2. Subsidy Assistance Pattern

| Farmer Category | Landholding Size | Minimum Central + State Subsidy Share | Farmer Contribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Small & Marginal Farmers (SC/ST/General/Women)** | Up to 2 Hectares (5 Acres) | **55% of Indicative Unit Cost** (In many states like Maharashtra, Gujarat, AP, Tamil Nadu, enhanced up to **70% to 80%**) | 20% to 45% |
| **Other / Large Farmers** | More than 2 Hectares | **45% of Indicative Unit Cost** (Enhanced to 55-60% in select states) | 40% to 55% |

- Subsidies apply to certified BIS-standard drip laterals, inline drippers, micro-sprinklers, mini-sprinklers, rainguns, sand filters, screen filters, and venturi injectors.
- Maximum limit: Financial assistance is provided for up to 5 hectares per beneficiary family.

---

## 3. Crop-Wise Suitability of Micro-Irrigation Systems
1. **Drip Irrigation (In-line / On-line):**
   - Horticultural Crops: Banana, Pomegranate, Citrus, Grapes, Mango, Guava.
   - Cash Field Crops: Sugarcane, Cotton, Maize, Chilli, Tomato, Onion, Potato.
2. **Micro-Sprinkler Systems:**
   - Close-growing vegetables (Garlic, Onion nursery, leafy greens, nursery beds, ginger, turmeric).
3. **Portable / Semi-Permanent Sprinkler Sets:**
   - Field crops: Wheat, Gram, Mustard, Soybean, Groundnut, Pulses.
4. **Raingun Sprinklers:**
   - Tall dense crops like Sugarcane and Fodder fields.

---

## 4. Application Process
- Register online through the State Horticulture / Agriculture Department portal (e.g., MahaDBT in Maharashtra, Horticulture Portal in MP/UP, e-Dharti in Rajasthan).
- **Documents Required:**
  - 7/12 / Khasra-Khatauni land record.
  - Aadhaar Card and Bank Passbook copy.
  - Electricity bill or proof of water source (well, borewell, canal, farm pond).
  - Quotation and site design map prepared by registered empanelled drip manufacturer (Jain Irrigation, Netafim, EPC Mahindra, Finolex, etc.).
- Pre-sanction inspection is conducted by Block Horticulture Officer, followed by installation and direct subsidy release to farmer's DBT account.
