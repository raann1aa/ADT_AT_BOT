# Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) Guide

## 1. Scheme Overview & Financial Benefit
- **Objective:** Central Sector scheme launched by the Government of India to provide income support to all landholding farmer families across India.
- **Financial Benefit:** **Rs. 6,000 per year** transferred directly into the bank accounts of eligible farmer families in **three equal four-monthly installments of Rs. 2,000 each**:
  - Period 1: April to July
  - Period 2: August to November
  - Period 3: December to March

---

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- All landholding farmer families having cultivable landholding registered in their names are eligible.
- **Who is NOT Eligible (Exclusion Criteria):**
  1. Institutional landholders.
  2. Former and present holders of constitutional posts.
  3. Former and present Ministers, MPs, MLAs, MLCs, Mayors, Chairpersons of District Panchayats.
  4. Serving or retired officers and employees of Central/State Govt. Ministries, Departments, and PSUs (excluding Class IV / Multi-Tasking Staff).
  5. All superannuated/retired pensioners whose monthly pension is Rs. 10,000 or more.
  6. All persons who paid Income Tax in the last assessment year.
  7. Professionals like Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Chartered Accountants, and Architects registered with professional bodies.

---

## 3. Mandatory Prerequisites for Receiving Installments
If an installment is stopped or pending, check these three mandatory conditions on the PM-KISAN portal (pmkisan.gov.in):
1. **eKYC Completed:**
   - **OTP-based eKYC:** Available free on the PM-KISAN portal / mobile app using Aadhaar-linked mobile number.
   - **Biometric eKYC:** Can be done at nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) via fingerprint/iris scan.
   - **Face Authentication eKYC:** Done via the PM-KISAN Mobile App using Govt. of India Aadhaar FaceRD service (no OTP or fingerprint required; family members can assist elders).
2. **Land Seeding (भूमि सत्यापन):**
   - Land record (Khata/Khasra/Khatauni) must be digitally verified and seeded with the farmer's PM-KISAN profile. If showing "Land Seeding: NO", submit copy of Land Ownership Deed (Khatauni) and Aadhaar to the local Patwari / Lekhpal / District Agriculture Officer.
3. **Aadhaar-Bank Account Seeding (NPCI Direct Benefit Transfer):**
   - Bank account must be linked to Aadhaar through the NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India) mapper for DBT. If inactive, visit bank branch to submit Aadhaar-DBT consent form, or open an Indian Post Payments Bank (IPPB) account (seeded automatically within 48 hours).
