# PM-KUSUM (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan) Scheme

## 1. Scheme Overview
Launched by Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE), PM-KUSUM aims to de-dieselise the farm sector, provide daytime uninterrupted power for irrigation, and enable farmers to sell surplus solar power back to the grid.

---

## 2. The Three Components of PM-KUSUM

### Component A: Solar Power Plants on Barren/Cultivable Land (0.5 MW to 2 MW)
- Individual farmers, cooperatives, or panchayats can set up small grid-connected solar power plants on unused or barren land within a 5 km radius of a rural sub-station.
- Power generated is purchased by the local DISCOM at pre-determined feed-in tariffs (FiT) for 25 years, providing steady cash rent/income to landholders.

### Component B: Standalone Solar Agricultural Pumps (Off-Grid) — *Most Popular with Farmers*
- Installation of standalone solar-powered agriculture pumps of up to **7.5 HP capacity** in off-grid rural areas where grid electricity is unavailable.
- **Subsidy Sharing Pattern:**
  - **Central Govt. Subsidy (CFA):** 30% of benchmark cost.
  - **State Govt. Subsidy:** 30% of benchmark cost (in many states like Haryana, MP, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, State contribution is increased to 40-45%).
  - **Farmer's Own Contribution:** **Only 10% to 40%** of pump cost! (Bank loan available for the farmer share).
- Replaces polluting, costly diesel pumps (saves Rs. 40,000 to 70,000 per year on diesel fuel).

### Component C: Solarisation of Existing Grid-Connected Agriculture Pumps
- Solarisation of existing electric agricultural tube wells.
- Farmers use solar power for irrigation during the day and sell excess unutilized electricity back to the DISCOM (KUSUM Feeder Solarisation).

---

## 3. Eligibility & Required Documents
- **Eligibility:** Farmers having legal agricultural landholding with an existing groundwater irrigation source (borewell, open dug well, farm pond) and valid water extraction permission.
- **Documents Required:**
  1. Aadhaar Card and mobile number linked with Aadhaar.
  2. Land ownership record (7/12 extract / Jamabandi / Khasra-Khatauni).
  3. Bank passbook copy (showing IFSC and account number).
  4. Certificate from Electricity DISCOM stating that the field has no existing agricultural power connection (for Component B).
  5. Water source verification report (borewell depth, casing pipe diameter).

---

## 4. How to Apply & Beware of Fake Portals
- Apply **ONLY through the official State Renewable Energy Development Agency portal** (e.g., MEDA in Maharashtra, MPUVNL in MP, RRECL in Rajasthan, Saral Haryana in Haryana, UPNEDA in UP).
- *Warning:* Beware of fraudulent private websites and unauthorized agents asking for registration fees. No fees are paid outside official state government payment gateways.
