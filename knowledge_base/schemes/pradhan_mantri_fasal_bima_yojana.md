# Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) Guide

## 1. Scheme Objectives & Coverage
PMFBY provides comprehensive insurance protection against yield loss, localized natural calamities, post-harvest losses, and unpreventable sowing failures due to adverse weather:
- **Eligible Risks Covered:**
  1. Prevented Sowing / Planting risk (deficit rainfall or adverse seasonal conditions).
  2. Standing Crop loss (non-preventable natural risks: drought, dry spells, flood, inundation, wide pest attack, landslides, natural fire, lightning, storm, hailstorm, cyclone).
  3. Post-Harvest losses (for crops kept in "cut & spread" condition in the field for up to 14 days after harvesting for drying against cyclone, unseasonal rains, and hailstorm).
  4. Localized Calamities (hailstorm, landslide, inundation, cloud burst, and natural fire).

---

## 2. Farmer Premium Contribution Rates
The farmer pays only a small nominal premium; the entire remaining actuarial premium is subsidized equally (50:50) by the Central and State Governments:

| Crop Category | Season | Farmer's Maximum Premium Share |
|---|---|---|
| **All Kharif Foodgrains & Oilseeds** (Paddy, Maize, Soybean, Bajra, etc.) | Kharif | **Only 2.0%** of Sum Insured |
| **All Rabi Foodgrains & Oilseeds** (Wheat, Gram, Mustard, Barley, etc.) | Rabi | **Only 1.5%** of Sum Insured |
| **Commercial / Annual Horticultural Crops** (Cotton, Sugarcane, Onion, Chilli, Potato, etc.) | Both Seasons | **Only 5.0%** of Sum Insured |

## 3. Critical: 72-Hour Claim Notification Rule for Localized Losses
For localized losses (hailstorm, sudden inundation/flood, cloudburst, landslide, post-harvest rain damage):
- **Golden Rule:** The farmer **MUST report the crop damage within 72 HOURS** of the occurrence of the calamity. If reported after 72 hours, the localized individual claim may be rejected!
- **How to Report Within 72 Hours:**
  1. **Crop Insurance Mobile App (फसल बीमा ऐप):** Open the app, click "Crop Loss Intimation", take geotagged photos of damaged crop in the field, enter survey/khasra number, and submit instantly. (Generates unique Docket Number).
  2. **National Crop Insurance Portal Toll-Free Helpline:** Call **14447** (dedicated national farmer toll-free number).
  3. **Offline Mode:** Submit written notification to the Bank Branch where insurance premium was debited, nearest Agriculture Officer, or Insurance Company representative within 72 hours.

---

## 4. Assessment and Payout Process
- **Localized Calamities:** Joint survey conducted within 10 days by Insurance Company representative and State Agriculture Department official using GPS-enabled tablets. Compensation credited directly to farmer's bank account within 15 days of survey report.
- **Widespread Calamity (Area Approach):** Assessed through State Govt. **Crop Cutting Experiments (CCE)** at village panchayat level. If actual yield falls below threshold yield, payout is triggered automatically to all insured farmers in that notified insurance unit without individual claims.
