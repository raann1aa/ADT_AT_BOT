# Soil Health Card (SHC) Scheme Guide

## 1. Scheme Overview
- Launched by Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare to issue soil health cards to every farmer once every 3 years.
- Provides crop-wise customized fertilizer and manure recommendations based on actual soil nutrient status, preventing wasteful over-application of Urea and DAP and saving Rs. 3,000 to 5,000 per acre in input costs.

---

## 2. The 12 Parameters Analyzed in Soil Health Card
The card reports the status of 12 critical chemical and physical indicators:
1. **Physical Status:** pH (soil reaction: acidic, neutral, or alkaline).
2. **Electrical Conductivity (EC):** Measures soluble salt concentration / salinity.
3. **Organic Carbon (OC):** Indicator of soil fertility, microbial health, and water holding capacity (<0.5% is Low, 0.5-0.75% is Medium, >0.75% is High).
4. **Primary Macronutrients (3):** Available Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), and Potassium (K).
5. **Secondary Nutrient (1):** Available Sulphur (S).
6. **Micronutrients (5):** Available Zinc (Zn), Iron (Fe), Copper (Cu), Manganese (Mn), and Boron (B).

## 3. How to Take an Accurate Soil Sample (मृदा नमूना लेने की सही विधि)
A poor sample gives misleading fertilizer advice. Follow this scientific procedure:

1. **Sampling Time:** After harvest of Rabi or Kharif crop when the field is empty, or 1 month before sowing. Do not sample immediately after fertilizer or manure application.
2. **Tools:** Khurpi, spade, auger, clean plastic bucket, and clean plastic/cloth bag. (Do not use fertilizer bags!).
3. **Sampling Pattern:** Walk in a **Zig-Zag "Z" pattern** across the field. Collect sub-samples from 8 to 10 different random spots across a uniform 1-hectare plot.
4. **Sampling Depth:**
   - Field Crops (Wheat, Paddy, Mustard, Pulses): 0 to 15 cm (0 to 6 inches).
   - Fruit Orchards: 0-30 cm, 30-60 cm, and 60-90 cm deep.
5. **"V" Shape Cut Method:**
   - Scrape away dry weeds and surface litter.
   - Dig a "V" shaped pit 15 cm deep with a spade.
   - Slice a 1-inch thick vertical layer of soil from top to bottom along one side of the "V" cut and place into the bucket.
6. **Quartering Method to Reduce Sample Size:**
   - Mix all 10 sub-samples thoroughly in the bucket.
   - Spread on a clean sheet into a circular heap and divide into 4 equal quarters.
   - Discard two opposite quarters; remix the remaining two quarters.
   - Repeat until approximately **500 grams of clean composite soil** remains.
7. **Labeling:** Pack in a clean bag with tag showing: Farmer's Name, Village, Khasra Number, Mobile Number, and Previous / Next Crop.

## 4. Submitting and Accessing the Card
- Submit to nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK), Block Agriculture Office, or Mobile Soil Testing Van.
- Farmers can view, download, and print their digital Soil Health Card anytime from the national portal: **https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in** using their state, district, village, and name.
