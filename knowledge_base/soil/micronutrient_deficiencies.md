# Micronutrient Deficiencies Identification & Correction

## 1. Zinc (Zn) Deficiency (जिंक की कमी)
Most widespread micronutrient deficiency in Indian soils (>40% soils deficient).
- **Classic Crop: Paddy (Khaira Disease / खैरा रोग):**
  - **Symptoms:** At 2-3 weeks after transplanting, lower leaves develop rusty brown/bronze spots starting between veins. Plants become severely stunted, tillering halts, and roots turn dark brown.
- **In Maize:** Causes "White Bud" — young emerging whorl leaves turn chalky white or bleached yellow.
- **In Wheat:** Mid-rib chlorosis and yellowish-white bands on leaves.

### Correction Recipe
- **Soil Application:** Apply **Zinc Sulphate Heptahydrate (21%) @ 10 kg/acre** OR **Monohydrate (33%) @ 7 kg/acre** at sowing.
  - *Warning:* NEVER mix Zinc Sulphate directly with DAP or SSP in the same bucket! Zinc reacts with phosphate to form insoluble Zinc Phosphate, rendering both nutrients unavailable to crops.
- **Emergency Foliar Spray:** Dissolve 500 g Zinc Sulphate (21%) + 1 kg slaked lime (चूना) OR 500 g Urea in 100 liters of water per acre. Alternatively, spray **Chelated Zinc (Zn-EDTA 12%) @ 1 g per liter of water**.

## 2. Boron (B) Deficiency (बोरॉन की कमी)
Boron is essential for pollen tube growth, fertilization, cell wall formation, and sugar translocation:
- **Symptoms:**
  - **Fruit Cracking:** Severe vertical splitting and cracking in tomato, pomegranate, and watermelons.
  - **Hollow / Black Heart:** Internal browning and hollow centers in cauliflower, potato tubers, and radish.
  - **Poor Pod / Grain Set:** In pulses and mustard, flowers bloom normally but drop off without setting pods (barren flowers).
- **Correction Recipe:**
  - Soil: Borax @ 4 kg/acre.
  - Foliar Spray: Spray **Di-Sodium Octaborate Tetrahydrate (Solubor 20%) @ 1 g to 1.5 g per liter of water** at flower bud initiation and early fruit set. Do not overdose boron as excess causes leaf tip scorching.

## 3. Iron (Fe) Deficiency (लोहे की कमी)
Common in high-pH calcareous soils (soils containing free calcium carbonate):
- **Symptoms:** **Interveinal chlorosis on the youngest upper leaves.** The leaf blade turns bright ivory white to yellow while veins remain sharply green. Unlike Nitrogen deficiency (which starts on old lower leaves), Iron deficiency always starts on top new leaves.
- **Correction Recipe:**
  - Foliar spray of **Ferrous Sulphate (FeSO4 19%) @ 5 g/L + 1 g Citric acid (नींबू का सत्त)** per liter of water OR
  - **Chelated Iron (Fe-EDTA 12%) @ 1 g per liter of water**.

---

## 4. Sulphur (S) Deficiency (सल्फर / गंधक की कमी)
Crucial for oil synthesis in oilseeds and pungency in onion and garlic:
- **Symptoms:** Young leaves turn uniform pale yellow (similar to nitrogen, but starts on upper leaves). In mustard, plants become spindly with cupped leaves and pinkish-purple undersides.
- **Correction Recipe:**
  - Apply **Bentonite Sulphur (90% pastilles) @ 10-15 kg/acre** at land preparation OR
  - Single Super Phosphate (SSP) as the phosphorus source.
