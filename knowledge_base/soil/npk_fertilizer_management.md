# NPK Chemical Fertilizer Management Guide

## 1. Primary Macronutrients & Their Functions
- **Nitrogen (N):** Promotes vegetative growth, green foliage, tillering, and chlorophyll synthesis. (Deficiency: General pale yellowing of older lower leaves).
- **Phosphorus (P):** Stimulates vigorous root development, early seedling vigor, flower initiation, and sturdy stem strength. (Deficiency: Purple/reddish discoloration on leaves and stems).
- **Potassium (K):** Confers disease resistance, regulates stomatal water balance, prevents lodging, and improves grain filling, fruit size, weight, sweetness, and shelf-life. (Deficiency: Scorching and browning of leaf margins).

## 3. Best Practices for Application
1. **Never Broadcast Phosphorus on Soil Surface:** Phosphorus is immobile in soil. Always place DAP or SSP 5 cm below and beside the seed during sowing. Surface-broadcast phosphorus gets fixed and wasted.
2. **SSP is Superior for Oilseeds & Pulses:** Because Single Super Phosphate provides 11% free sulphur and 19% calcium in addition to phosphorus, it gives significantly higher yields than DAP in mustard, groundnut, soybean, onion, and pulses.
3. **Split Nitrogen Applications:**
   - Never apply all urea at sowing.
   - For cereals: 1/3rd at basal sowing, 1/3rd at active tillering, and 1/3rd at panicle/jointing stage.
   - Avoid top-dressing urea on dry soil or in bright midday sun (causes ammonia volatilization loss). Apply in moist soil during early morning or late afternoon.

## 4. Water-Soluble Fertilizers (WSF) for Drip & Foliar Sprays
- **19:19:19 (General Vegetative Boost):** Spray @ 5 g/L during active vegetative stage (20-40 days).
- **12:61:00 (Mono Ammonium Phosphate - MAP):** Spray @ 4-5 g/L at early root establishment and flower bud formation.
- **00:52:34 (Mono Potassium Phosphate - MKP):** Spray @ 5 g/L at peak flowering and fruit/pod set (strengthens stalks and arrests flower drop).
- **13:00:45 (Potassium Nitrate):** Spray @ 7-10 g/L during fruit enlargement and grain filling (enhances size and color).
- **00:00:50 (Potassium Sulphate - SOP):** Spray @ 5 g/L for sugar accumulation, shining skin in onion/potato, and superior keeping quality.
