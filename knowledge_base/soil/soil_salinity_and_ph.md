# Soil pH, Salinity & Alkalinity Reclamation Guide

## 1. Understanding Soil pH Scale
- **Acidic Soils (pH < 6.5):** Found in heavy rainfall zones (Assam, Kerala, coastal Karnataka, Odisha hills). Iron and Aluminum toxicity; Phosphorus and Calcium deficiency.
- **Normal / Neutral Soils (pH 6.5 to 7.5):** Optimum zone where all 16 plant nutrients have maximum plant availability.
- **Saline Soils (खारी मिट्टी - EC > 4 dS/m, pH < 8.5):** Excessive soluble neutral salts (chlorides and sulphates of Na, Ca, Mg). White salt crust appears on surface during dry spells.
- **Alkali / Sodic Soils (ऊसर / रेह - pH > 8.5, ESP > 15%):** Dominated by sodium carbonate and bicarbonate. Black greasy crust, soil dispersion, no water infiltration, sticky when wet, rock-hard when dry.

---

## 2. Reclaiming Alkali / Sodic (ऊसर) Soils with Gypsum (जिप्सम)
Gypsum (Calcium Sulphate - CaSO4·2H2O) replaces toxic exchangeable Sodium ions with soluble Calcium ions:

### Step-by-Step Reclamation Method
1. **Soil Testing:** Test soil to find Gypsum Requirement (GR). Standard dose ranges from 2.5 to 5 tonnes of agricultural-grade gypsum per acre depending on sodicity.
2. **Land Leveling & Bunding:** Level field thoroughly and construct strong 30 cm peripheral bunds to hold water.
3. **Application:** Broadcast powdered gypsum uniformly in May-June and mix shallowly in top 10 cm soil with cultivator.
4. **Ponding & Leaching:** Pond fresh canal/rain water for 15-20 days. Calcium displaces Sodium, and soluble Sodium Sulphate is leached down into the subsoil.
5. **Cropping:** Sown with salt-tolerant green manure crop **Dhaincha (*Sesbania aculeata*)**. Incorporate 45-day-old Dhaincha into the soil, followed by transplanting salt-tolerant paddy varieties (CSR 30, CSR 36, Narendra Usar 3).

---

## 3. Reclaiming Saline (लवणीय) Soils
- Saline soils have neutral salts (no excess sodium on clay exchange). **Gypsum is NOT required for saline soils!**
- **Remedy:** Provide subsurface tile drainage or open drainage ditches (1-1.5 m deep) at 20-30 m intervals. Flood with good quality canal water to leach soluble salts down and out through the drainage network.
- Grow salt-tolerant crops: Barley, Mustard, Cotton, Beetroot, Spinach.

---

## 4. Correcting Acidic Soils (अम्लीय मिट्टी सुधार)
- Apply Agricultural Lime (Calcium Carbonate - CaCO3) or Dolomite @ 1 to 2 tonnes/acre during summer ploughing.
- Lime neutralizes toxic aluminum/manganese, raises soil pH towards 6.5, and activates beneficial nitrogen-fixing bacteria.
