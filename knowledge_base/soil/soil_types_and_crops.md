# Major Indian Soil Types & Crop Suitability

## 1. Alluvial Soil (जलोढ़ मिट्टी)
- **Regions:** Indo-Gangetic plains (Punjab, Haryana, UP, Bihar, West Bengal, coastal deltas).
- **Properties:** Formed by silt deposited by rivers. Highly fertile, rich in potash and lime, light to medium texture, neutral pH (6.5 to 7.5). Deficient in nitrogen and organic carbon.
- **Suitable Crops:** Wheat, Paddy/Rice, Sugarcane, Maize, Potato, Mustard, Vegetables, Pulses.
- **Management:** Requires balanced NPK fertilization, regular addition of organic manure (FYM), and green manuring (dhaincha/sunnhemp) to maintain soil organic carbon.

---

## 2. Black Cotton Soil / Regur (काली मिट्टी)
- **Regions:** Deccan plateau (Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, northern Karnataka, parts of Andhra/Telangana).
- **Properties:** High clay content (montmorillonite), rich in Calcium, Magnesium, and Potash. Deficient in Nitrogen, Phosphorus, and Zinc.
- **Characteristics:** **High swelling and shrinkage:** Extremely sticky when wet, develops wide, deep cracks when dry (self-ploughing property). Very high water retention capacity.
- **Suitable Crops:** Cotton, Soybean, Gram (Chana), Wheat, Sugarcane, Onion, Citrus, Pomegranate.
- **Management:**
  - Highly susceptible to waterlogging during heavy monsoon rains; adopt Broad Bed Furrow (BBF) or ridge-furrow layout.
  - Apply Single Super Phosphate (SSP) for phosphorus rather than rock phosphate.
  - Work soil when in workable moisture condition (vapsa/tar-wattar); ploughing dry or sodden black soil destroys structure.

---

## 3. Red and Yellow Soil (लाल मिट्टी)
- **Regions:** Southern plateau (Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Odisha, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, Bundelkhand).
- **Properties:** Derived from crystalline igneous rocks; red color is due to iron oxides (ferric diffusion). Poor in nitrogen, phosphorus, humus, and lime. Mildly acidic to neutral (pH 5.5 to 6.8).
- **Characteristics:** Coarse to loamy texture, free-draining, low water holding capacity.
- **Suitable Crops:** Groundnut, Millets (Ragi, Bajra), Pulses, Tobacco, Tomato, Mango.
- **Management:** Frequent light irrigations, application of heavy FYM/compost, and phosphorus banding near the seed zone.

---

## 4. Sandy / Arid Soil (बलुई / रेतीली मिट्टी)
- **Regions:** Western Rajasthan, southern Haryana, north Gujarat.
- **Properties:** Coarse texture, very low clay and organic matter, alkaline pH, high permeability, very poor water and nutrient retention (leaching prone).
- **Suitable Crops:** Pearl millet (Bajra), Guar (Cluster bean), Moth bean, Mustard, Gram, Cumin (Jeera), Isabgol.
- **Management:** Drip irrigation is essential; split fertilizer doses into 4-5 small applications rather than large basal doses to prevent nitrogen leaching.
