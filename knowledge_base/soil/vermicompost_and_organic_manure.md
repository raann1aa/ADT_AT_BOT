# Vermicompost, FYM & Biofertilizer Management Guide

## 1. Well-Decomposed Farm Yard Manure (FYM - गोबर की सड़ी खाद)
- **Quality Check:** Mature FYM should be dark brown to black, crumbly, odorless (no ammonia or foul dung smell), with moisture around 25-30%.
  - *Warning:* Applying raw, fresh, undecomposed cattle dung introduces termite infestations, weed seeds, and white grub pests, and causes nitrogen immobilization (dung burns crop roots).
- **Recommended Doses:**
  - Field Crops (Wheat, Paddy, Maize): 4 to 6 tonnes per acre every 2 years.
  - Cash Vegetables (Tomato, Chilli, Onion, Potato): 8 to 10 tonnes per acre before bed formation.
  - Fruit Orchards: 25 to 40 kg per mature tree basin during winter dormancy.

---

## 2. Vermicompost (केंचुआ खाद)
Vermicompost is organic matter processed by earthworms. It contains 5 times more available Nitrogen, 7 times more Phosphorus, and 11 times more Potassium than topsoil, along with auxins, gibberellins, and beneficial microflora.
- **Best Earthworm Species:** *Eisenia fetida* (Red Californian earthworm) or *Eudrilus eugeniae* (African nightcrawler).
- **Bed Preparation:**
  - Construct beds 30 feet long, 3-4 feet wide, and 2 feet high under thatched shade.
  - Base layer: 3 inches dry stalks/straw.
  - Middle layer: 15-day pre-decomposed cattle dung mixed with crop residues (60:40 ratio).
  - Release 1 kg worms (approx. 1000 worms) per linear meter of bed.
  - Maintain 60-70% moisture by sprinkling water every 2-3 days.
  - Harvest dark granular casts (like tea leaves) in 50-60 days.
- **Field Doses:**
  - Vegetables & Onions: 1.5 to 2.0 tonnes per acre.
  - Cereals & Pulses: 1.0 tonne per acre at sowing.
  - Potted plants & Kitchen gardens: 100-200 g per pot monthly.

## 3. Microbial Biofertilizers (जैव उर्वरक)

| Biofertilizer | Mechanism | Target Crops | Method of Application |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rhizobium** | Symbiotic nitrogen fixation in root nodules | All Pulses (Gram, Moong, Urad, Soybean, Pea) | Seed treatment @ 10 g/kg seed |
| **Azotobacter** | Free-living nitrogen fixer | Non-legumes (Wheat, Paddy, Mustard, Cotton, Maize) | Seed treatment @ 10 g/kg or seedling dip |
| **Azospirillum** | Associative nitrogen fixer | Sugarcane, Sorghum, Millets | Soil application 2 kg/acre with FYM |
| **PSB (Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria)** | Secretes organic acids to dissolve fixed insoluble soil phosphate | All Crops | Seed treatment @ 10 g/kg or 2 kg/acre in soil |
| **KMB (Potash Mobilizing Bacteria)** | Mobilizes fixed mineral potassium (*Frateuria aurantia*) | Heavy K feeders (Potato, Banana, Sugarcane) | 1 liter/acre via drip irrigation |
